What does “learning” mean?

A model adjusts numerical parameters so its predictions better match training examples.

Temperature, vibration

Training: adjust the weights

Measure error with a loss function. Use gradients and an optimizer to update the model.

Inference: use the weights

Apply the trained model to a new input. A prediction does not normally update the model.

The goal is useful predictions on unseen examples, not memorising the training data.

AI GPU computingThe hardware behind the workload

Graphics Processing Unit

Why AI uses GPUs

Neural networks perform many similar numerical operations. GPUs can execute large amounts of this work in parallel.

  • Parallel work: matrix operations across batches of examples.
  • GPU memory: holds weights, activations and, during training, gradients and optimizer state.
  • Device consistency: model and input tensors must be on compatible devices.

We will check device availability and choose model and batch sizes that fit the available memory.

A GPU can speed up suitable workloads. It cannot fix poor data or guarantee a correct answer.

Course focus: using GPU-backed notebooks, not designing GPU kernels.06 / 12

What we will observe

A batch is a group of examples.
An epoch is one pass through the training set.

Falling training loss alone is not proof of better predictions. Compare a baseline and inspect validation errors.

PrecisionOf the cases predicted positive, how many really were positive?
RecallOf the actual positive cases, how many did we find?
F1 scoreThe harmonic mean of precision and recall.

If 95 of 100 samples are normal, always predicting “normal” gets 95% accuracy and detects zero faults.

Choose settings on validation data; use the held-out test set for final evaluation.07 / 12
AI TransformersFrom tokens to context

An architecture behind many modern language models

Words depend on their context

Attention lets a model combine information from relevant positions in a sequence.

Themotorstoppedbecauseitoverheated

“It” refers to “motor” in this sentence. Context matters.
Word-level illustration only; actual tokenizers may split words into smaller pieces.

Tokens

Text is converted into token IDs. Tokens may be words, subwords or other units.

Embeddings & positions

Numerical representations and position information make the sequence usable by the model.

Attention & output

Representations are updated using context. Task-specific outputs may be labels or generated text.

A context window is limited. Fluent generated text can still be factually wrong.

We will use small pretrained models, not pretrain a large language model.08 / 12

AI The individual projectWeek 04 / Evaluate & present

A document assistant you can explain and test

A useful answer needs evidence

Choose approved engineering documents: a lab manual, equipment guide or technical reference relevant to your discipline.

Build

A working RAG notebook

Document preparation, retrieval, answers and source references. Keep a source manifest and reproducible execution steps.

Test

Ten deliberate questions

6 factual + 2 multi-passage + 2 unanswerable. Check retrieval, correctness, evidence support and response time.

Show

Results, not just a demo

Compare one chunk-size or top-k change. Submit your notebook, evaluation table, 2-3 page report and five-slide presentation.

Responsible use: avoid private data; treat document instructions as untrusted; verify that citations support the claims. A citation alone is not proof.

Keep a small question subset unseen until final assessment. Discuss limitations and at least one failure case.
